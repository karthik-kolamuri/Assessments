⚖️ Why 0ms Boundary Collar?

Traditional benchmarks use a 250ms collar that forgives 500ms of boundary uncertainty per turn. Modern voice bots cannot tolerate cross-talk hallucinations, so Voice Arena scores at true acoustic onset/offset.

👥 Unknown Speaker Count

No model is provided the number of speakers upfront. Systems must autonomously cluster x-vectors and discover the speaker count, mirroring production reality.

📊 Bootstrap Significance

All scores are computed with 1,000 bootstrap iterations. Pairwise rankings are confirmed with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (p < 0.01) to eliminate ranking by chance.
